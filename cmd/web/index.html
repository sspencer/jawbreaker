How to Play Jawbreaker

Jawbreaker is a puzzle game where you remove groups of connected balls of the same color.

Rules:

  • Click on a group of two or more connected balls of the same color to remove them.
  • After removing balls, the remaining balls will fall down to fill empty spaces.
  • If a column becomes empty, columns to the right will shift left to fill the gap.
  • The more balls you remove at once, the higher your score.
  • The game ends when there are no more valid moves.
  • If you clear most of the board, you'll get bonus points!

Scoring:

Score = n × (n-1) where n is the number of balls removed.

Bonus points are awarded if you finish with fewer than 20 balls remaining.
